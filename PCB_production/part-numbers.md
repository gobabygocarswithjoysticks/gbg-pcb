### A: battery connector
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: screw terminal (J5)
#### 2: tabs for Weelye mode (J102 J103)

### B: left motor connector
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: screw terminal (J7)
#### 2: tabs for Weelye mode (J100 J101)

### C: right motor connector
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: screw terminal
#### 2: Molex 2159151002 (J104)
#### 3: TE 1-1123723-2 (J105)

### D: pico
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: Pico 1
#### 2: Pico 2
#### 1W: Pico 1W (wifi)
#### 2W: Pico 2W (wifi)

### E: voltage regulator
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: default (U3, C3, R5, C6, L1, C7, R8, R9, C8)

### F: button ports
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: soldered (B1, B2, B3, B4)

### G: I2C connector
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: soldered (J2)

### H: battery and current monitor
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: soldered (U4, R11, R12, R13, R14, R20, C4)

### J: speed knob
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: soldered (J4)

### K: case
#### 0: no case
#### 1: default case
#### 2: case with 12V fans 

### L: resistor divider for 5V tolerant pins on 5,6,7,8
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: installed (R10)
#### 2: output enabled

### M:  extra GPIO pins
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: installed (J1)

### N: power switch and charging pins
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: installed (J11)

### P: switch terminal
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: installed (SW1)

### Q: power terminal
#### 0: not installed
#### 1: installed (J6)
