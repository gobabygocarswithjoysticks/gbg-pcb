To protect your GBG-PCB, print the top and bottom parts. 

Assemble using 4 12mm M3 bolts and 4 M3 nuts.

To add fans (fans can be used to cool the boards and increase the maximum current that the boards can supply), add the following parts:
* Print the fan-funnel part and the fan-cover part
* Get 2 cooling fans (choose voltage compatible with your car)
    * [5V, 3.5-6](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/sunon-fans/MF40200V1-1000U-A99/6198712), or 
    * [12V, 4.5-13.8](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/sunon-fans/MF40201V1-1000U-A99/6198971), or 
    * [24V 8-27.6](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/sunon-fans/MF40202V1-1000U-A99/6198716)
* Assemble using 4 40mm M4 bolts and 4 M4 nuts
    * Bolt the fans to the bottom piece of the case before assembling the top and bottom parts around the GBG-PCB.
